Child Safety Standards Policy (CSAE Prevention)

fandrop maintains a zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

1. Explicit Prohibition

Users are strictly prohibited from creating, uploading, sharing, storing, or transmitting any content that facilitates, depicts, or promotes child sexual exploitation or abuse. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in any form (photos, videos, illustrations, or text).
  • Child grooming, online solicitation, or attempts to arrange sexual contact with minors.
  • Sextortion, extortion, or blackmail targeting minors.
  • Sexualization or exploitation of children in any context.

2. Enforcement and Content Removal

Upon acquiring actual knowledge or receiving a report of potential CSAE or CSAM, fandrop will take immediate action, including:

  • Immediate removal and permanent purging of the offending content.
  • Immediate permanent suspension and banning of the user account.
  • Preservation of essential audit logs for legal compliance.

3. Legal Compliance & Mandatory Reporting

fandrop complies with all applicable child protection laws and regulations. Any confirmed or suspected instances of CSAM or child exploitation will be promptly reported to relevant law enforcement authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and Philippine regional law enforcement agencies.

4. In-App User Feedback & Reporting Mechanisms

Users can report any suspicious content, user profile, or interaction directly within the mobile application using the "Report" or "Report a Problem" button under Help & Safety. Reports related to child safety are prioritized for immediate review.

5. Designated Child Safety Point of Contact

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, our designated representative to handle child safety inquiries, reports, and notifications is accessible directly via:

Child Safety Contact Email: safety@fandrop.com.ph (or support@fandrop.com.ph)
